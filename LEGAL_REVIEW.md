# Chess Sagas legal review checklist

The in-app Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund and Cancellation Policy
are implementation-aligned drafts, not legal advice. They must not be treated as
attorney-approved until qualified counsel records approval below.

## Information to give counsel

- The operator's registered legal name, entity type, business address, and state
  or country of formation.
- Every jurisdiction in which the service will be marketed or sold.
- Whether users under 18 will be allowed and how age requirements will be
  enforced.
- The production data-flow and vendor list: Netlify, Heroku, the production
  database host, Stripe, Google, Apple, OpenAI, SMTP2GO, and web-push providers.
- Data retention and backup schedules for accounts, sagas, billing records,
  generation logs, support messages, sessions, and push subscriptions.
- The intended refund rules: unused one-time purchases may be requested within
  14 days; subscription payments are normally non-refundable and not prorated;
  non-waivable local consumer rights still apply.
- The intended credit rules: one credit per successful new saga, free completed
  saga reuse, no additional charge for internal retries, release after terminal
  failure, no current expiration, and rollover after subscription cancellation.
- The fact that generated sagas and submitted PGN metadata are public by default.
- The graphic-fiction content profile and the AI error disclosure.

## Decisions counsel must confirm

- Governing law, venue, dispute-resolution, arbitration, and class-action terms,
  if any.
- Whether regional cooling-off, automatic-renewal, tax, accessibility, privacy,
  or cancellation requirements require different product behavior or wording.
- Whether the account deletion workflow and public-saga removal process satisfy
  all applicable access, deletion, correction, portability, objection, and appeal
  rights.
- Whether the operator needs a separate cookie notice, consent manager, data
  processing agreements, international transfer mechanism, or state-specific
  privacy addendum.
- Whether the AI provider terms and the public-content license support the
  intended personal and commercial uses of generated sagas.
- Whether liability limits, warranty disclaimers, age language, and content rules
  are enforceable in the target jurisdictions.
